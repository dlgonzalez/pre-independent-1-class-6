Group Project

Design The Ultimate Guide

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. The teacher will assign your group an emergency: An Avalanche, A Forest Fire, A Sinking Ship, or An Earthquake.
  2. You are the experts. You have 15 minutes to write a 4-step "Ultimate Survival Guide" for this emergency.
  3. You MUST use target grammar: manage to, succeed in, able to, can/could.
  4. Present your guide to the class. Be commanding and confident!

Required Structure

  • Step 1: Immediate Action. What is the very first thing you must do?
  • Step 2: Protection. How do you stay safe from the immediate danger?
  • Step 3: Resources. What essential items do you need?
  • Step 4: Rescue. How do you manage to get help?

Grammar Scaffolding

  • "If you want to succeed in surviving, you must..."
  • "You have to manage to find a safe place."
  • "If you do this, you will be able to breathe."
  • "Make sure you can signal for help."
